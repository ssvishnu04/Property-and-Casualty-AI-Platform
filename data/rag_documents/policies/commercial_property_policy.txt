Commercial Property Insurance Policy

Coverage:
- Wind damage
- Hail damage
- Fire damage
- Theft and vandalism

Exclusions:
- Flood damage unless endorsed
- Intentional damage

Risk Indicators:
- Catastrophe exposure increases claim severity
- Repeated claims indicate higher risk
- Litigation involvement increases claim complexity

Claims Handling:
- High-value claims require senior adjuster review
- Catastrophe claims should be fast-tracked
